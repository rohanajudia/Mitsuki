How we keep quality

From raw material to dealer, in five steps.

  1. Choosing materials

    We select raw materials for each product.

  2. Testing

    Materials and finished products are tested.

  3. Blending

    Products are blended to their recipe.

  4. Packing

    Products are packed and labelled.

  5. Dispatch

    Orders are sent to dealers.
